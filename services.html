Services Offered

At Northern Support Services, our unwavering dedication is to provide a comprehensive range of support designed to meet the diverse needs of our clients.

Daily Living Support

  • Daily Living Support is essential for participants who require assistance with everyday activities to maintain their independence and quality of life.
  • Our service offers help with personal care, meal preparation, household chores, and other daily tasks tailored to the individual needs of each participant.
  • We aim to empower participants by promoting their ability to manage their daily lives while providing the necessary support to ensure their well-being and comfort.
  • . Whether it's helping with grooming, shopping, or household management, our team is committed to providing respectful, reliable, and practical support.

Social, Economic, and Community Engagement

  • We are committed to supporting participants in leading enriched and fulfilling lives by promoting active participation in social, economic, and community activities.
  • Our services help participants build and strengthen their social networks, access various community resources, and engage in employment, volunteer opportunities, or other economic activities that align with their interests and abilities.
  • Whether joining a local group, pursuing a hobby, or seeking employment, we provide the guidance, resources, and support necessary to enhance the participant's social life and community involvement.

Implementing Behaviour Support Plans

  • Our team of skilled professionals works closely with participants, their families, and other stakeholders to develop and implement tailored Behaviour Support Plans (BSPs).
  • These plans address challenging behaviours and promote positive changes, enhancing the participant's overall well-being and quality of life.
  • We take a person-centred approach, ensuring that the BSPs are grounded in evidence-based practices and tailored to each participant's individual needs and goals.
  • Our ongoing support includes regular monitoring, adjustment of strategies, and training for caregivers and support workers to ensure the plan's effectiveness.

Accommodation Assistance

  • Finding and maintaining appropriate accommodation is crucial to the well-being and independence of participants.
  • Our Accommodation Assistance services are designed to help participants navigate the complexities of securing suitable housing that meets their needs and preferences.
  • We assist with everything from understanding rental agreements to liaising with landlords, ensuring the accommodation is safe, accessible, and conducive to the participant’s goals.
  • We also offer support in managing housing-related issues so participants can enjoy stability and comfort in their living arrangements.

Supported Independent Living (SIL)

  • Supported Independent Living (SIL) is a service designed to help participants live as independently as possible, with support tailored to their individual needs.
  • We assist with everyday tasks, such as personal care, cooking, cleaning, and managing household responsibilities, within a shared or individual living arrangement.
  • We aim to foster independence while ensuring a safe and supportive living environment.
  • Whether participants require round-the-clock care or just a few hours of support each day, our dedicated team is here to help them achieve their personal goals and live a life of independence and dignity.

Respite Care

  • Respite Care provides essential relief to primary caregivers by offering temporary care for participants in a safe and supportive environment.
  • We understand the demands of caregiving and the importance of taking a break to recharge.
  • Our Respite Care services are designed to give caregivers peace of mind, knowing their loved ones receive high-quality care during their absence.
  • Whether for a few hours, overnight, or extended periods, our trained staff provides attentive, compassionate care tailored to the participant's needs, ensuring that their routine and comfort are maintained while their primary caregivers rest.

At Northern Support Services, we believe everyone deserves to live independently and with dignity. Our services are designed to help you achieve just that, offering the support you need to live confidently and comfortably in your home. We are here to empower you to live life on your terms. Please feel free to contact us for more information on the services we provide.
