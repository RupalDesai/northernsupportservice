We are registered under NDIS Provider in Northern Territory

At Northern Support Services, we stand out as more than just an NDIS provider. We are your partners in navigating the path to a better tomorrow. Our unique approach, grounded in respect, empathy, and empowerment, sets us apart. We understand that each person's needs are unique, so we tailor our services to fit the goals and aspirations of the individuals and families we serve.

A shared vision drives our dedicated team: to enhance the quality of life for our clients through innovative, responsive, and client-centered care. We pride ourselves on our strong local presence and deep understanding of the Northern Territory's unique challenges and opportunities. This enables us to deliver support that is not only effective but truly meaningful.

Our Services

Daily Living Support

Our Daily Living Support services focus on helping participants with everyday tasks, such as meal preparation, personal care, and household chores, enabling them to maintain a high quality of life.

Social, Economic, and Community Engagement

We empower participants to engage in social, economic, and community activities, helping them build relationships, access services, and enhance their overall well-being.

Implementing Behaviour Support Plans

Our team works with participants to develop and implement tailored behaviour support plans, ensuring they receive the necessary guidance to improve their quality of life and achieve their goals.

Accommodation Assistance

We assist participants in finding and maintaining suitable housing, ensuring that they live in environments that meet their needs and preferences, contributing to their overall stability and independence.

Respite Care

We offer Respite Care services to provide short-term relief for primary caregivers, ensuring that participants receive quality care and support while their caregivers take a well-deserved break.

Supported Independent Living (SIL)

We provide Supported Independent Living services to help participants live as independently as possible, assisting with daily tasks, personal care, and other essential support in a safe and comfortable environment.
